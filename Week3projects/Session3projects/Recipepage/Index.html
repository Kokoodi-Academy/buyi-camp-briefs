Pepperoni Pizza

A delicious homemade pizza with a crispy base, rich tomato sauce, melted mozzarella cheese, and flavorful pepperoni slices.

Preparation Time

  • Total time: 35 minutes
  • Preparation: 15 minutes
  • Cooking: 20 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 pizza dough
  • 1/2 cup pizza tomato sauce
  • 1 1/2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • pepperoni slices
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 220°C (425°F).
  2. Place the pizza dough on a lightly greased baking tray.
  3. Spread the tomato sauce evenly over the dough.
  4. Sprinkle the mozzarella cheese over the sauce.
  5. Arrange the pepperoni slices on top of the cheese.
  6. Add oregano, garlic powder, salt, and pepper.
  7. Drizzle lightly with olive oil.
  8. Bake for 15–20 minutes, until the crust is golden and the cheese is bubbling.
  9. Allow the pizza to cool for a few minutes, then slice and serve.
